TRUE SOURCE-TAGGED O2
=====================

Single 2-phase / 4-component run:
  H2O
  N2
  O2       = background oxygen
  O2tag    = source-tagged oxygen

Initial O2tag = 0 everywhere.

Only atmospheric O2 supplied through the recharge boundary while that
boundary is exposed to atmosphere during 101 <= t < 864 h is assigned
to O2tag.

Atmospheric O2 entering through the ordinary ground surface remains O2.

At t >= 864 h, no new O2tag is supplied. Existing O2tag continues to
move and react.

O2tag has the same O2:
  molar mass
  Henry coefficient
  molecular diffusion
  gas/liquid advection
  mechanical dispersion
  phase equilibrium
  liquid respiration rate = 0.06 h^-1
  gas respiration rate = 0.002 h^-1

Thus:
  O2_total = O2 + O2tag

Schedule:
0-96 h       wetting
96-101 h     smooth shutdown
101-864 h    atmospheric drainage; recharge-surface incoming O2 is O2tag
864-869 h    smooth rewetting; incoming water O2 is untagged O2
869-960 h    wetting

Output every 1 h.
